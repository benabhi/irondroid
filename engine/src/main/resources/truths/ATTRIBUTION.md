# World truths: attribution

The English texts in `truths.en.json` (each truth option and its quest starter) come from chapter 4,
"Your Truths", of the Ironsworn System Reference Document by Shawn Tomkin
(https://www.ironswornrpg.com), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes from the original:
- The Markdown copy names two categories "Leader" and "Beast"; they are loaded as "Leaders" and
  "Beasts", as in the rulebook.
- The d100 ranges of the options (1-33, 34-67, 68-100) are not in the SRD, which only asks the player
  to choose. They are added so the app can pick an option at random.
- A stray space before "including" (Legacies, third quest starter) is removed.

The Spanish texts in `truths.es.json` are an irondroid translation of those texts, under the same license.
