# Oracle table texts: attribution

The English table texts in `tables.en.json` come from the Ironsworn System Reference Document
by Shawn Tomkin (https://www.ironswornrpg.com), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The inspiration tables (IRON-103: Action, Theme, Region, Location, Location Descriptor, the Quick
Settlement Name Generator, Character Role, Goal and Descriptor, Ironlander Names and Major Plot Twist)
come from chapter 6 of the same SRD.

Changes from the original:
- The 91-94 range of Pay the Price is written `91=94` in the SRD Markdown and is loaded as 91-94.
- The Markdown copy writes "Battier Islands" for the Barrier Islands region and leaves out row 100
  of Character Role ("Unusual role" in the rulebook); both are loaded as in the rulebook.
- The Markdown copy splits the 200 Ironlander names in two tables labeled male and female names.
  The rulebook has a single gender-neutral table rolled on either half, so they are loaded as
  Ironlander Names (1-100) and (101-200).
- The Quick Settlement Name Generator is loaded as two tables, one for the prefix and one for the suffix.

The Spanish texts in `tables.es.json` are an irondroid translation of those texts, under the same license.
