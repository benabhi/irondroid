# Move texts: attribution

The English move texts in `moves.en.json` come from the Ironsworn System Reference Document
by Shawn Tomkin (https://www.ironswornrpg.com), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes from the original: typos fixed, and some outcomes split into separate options so the
engine can apply them (for example the weak hits of Heal and Resupply, and the miss of
Reach Your Destination).

The Spanish texts in `moves.es.json` are an irondroid translation of those texts, under the same license.
